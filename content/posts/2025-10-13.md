## OpenSpec 

> [OpenSpec](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec) is an open-source lightweight spec-driven framework for AI coding assistants

## Penpot 

> [Penpot](https://penpot.app/) is a free and open-source design and prototyping tool built with web standards, supporting collaboration between designers and developers.

## Requestly 

> [Requestly](https://requestly.com) is an open-source frontend development platform that acts as a local proxy, enabling developers to intercept, modify, and debug network traffic in real time.
