## Continue

> [Continue](https://www.continue.dev/) is an open-source AI coding assistant designed to integrate seamlessly into popular development environments like Visual Studio Code and JetBrains IDEs, aiming to enhance developer productivity through intelligent automation.

## PowerSync

> [PowerSync](https://www.powersync.com/) is an open-source sync engine that enables real-time, reactive, local-first and offline-first applications by automatically synchronizing data between a backend database and an on-device SQLite database.

## Hetzner

> [Hetzner](https://www.hetzner.com/) is a German web hosting and cloud computing company that provides a range of hosting services, including dedicated servers, virtual private servers (VPS), and cloud solutions. Known for its competitive pricing and high-performance infrastructure, Hetzner caters to both individual users and enterprises requiring scalable and reliable hosting solutions.

## Canine

> [Canine](https://canine.sh/) is an open-source deployment platform designed as a cost-effective alternative to Heroku, aiming to provide a similar user experience while avoiding vendor lock-in and high costs

## Portainer

> [Portainer](https://www.portainer.io.) is an open-source, lightweight management platform that provides a graphical user interface (GUI) for managing containerized applications across Docker, Kubernetes, and Podman environments.
