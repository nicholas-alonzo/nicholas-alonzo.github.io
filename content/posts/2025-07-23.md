## Lumo

> [Lumo](https://proton.me/blog/lumo-ai) is a privacy-first AI assistant developed by Proton, which ensures that user conversations are confidential and not used for training AI models. It uses zero-access encryption, meaning that chats are only readable on the user's device and not stored on Proton's servers.

## Passbolt

> [Passbolt](https://www.passbolt.com) is an open source credential platform for modern teams. A versatile, battle-tested solution to manage and collaborate on passwords, accesses, and secrets. All in one.
